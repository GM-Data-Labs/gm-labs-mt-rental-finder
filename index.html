How scoring works

Back taxes 1–4 points, scaled to the property's value (+1 if more than one year is owed) · trustee or sheriff sale scheduled +4 · trustee-sale notice recorded +2 · open city code case +2 (+1 if dangerous) · owned 20+ years +2, 10+ years +1 · owner out of state +2, elsewhere in Montana +1 · estate +2, trust +1. Every lead scores 4+ and has at least one hard signal (back taxes, a sale, a recorded notice or an open code case).

No owner names, by design. To see the owner of record, search the geocode on Montana Cadastral or the tax ID on the county treasurer's site.
